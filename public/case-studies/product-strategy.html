02 / Case study · Product Strategy

Turning under-used products into a growth engine

How aligning executive leadership around one product marketing and sales strategy drove organic sales roughly 50% above projections.

Capability
Product Strategy
Focus
Go-to-market alignment
Scope
Executive, cross-functional
Result
~50% above projected organic sales

The pattern

Large, matrixed organizations often own products with real value that never reach their potential. Product, marketing and sales each work from their own priorities, so the products get inconsistent positioning, uneven sales support, and no single owner of the growth story.

The opportunity is already built and paid for. It just isn't being used.

My approach

  • Aligned leadership across the matrix. I worked with executive leaders from different parts of the organization to agree on a shared view of the opportunity.
  • Built one strategy, not several. Instead of separate plans for each team, product marketing and sales moved forward on a single, unified strategy.
  • Started with what already existed. The strategy centered on existing but under-utilized products, unlocking growth from investments the organization had already made.

Impact

  • Organic sales came in approximately 50% above projections over the measurement period.
  • Growth came from products the organization already had.
