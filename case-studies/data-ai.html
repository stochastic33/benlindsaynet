Out-optimizing a specialist to win a bigger share of spend

How custom optimization algorithms, built to integrate with leading ad-buying platforms, delivered a 28% lift in conversions and outpaced a more specialized competitor.

Capability
Data & AI
Focus
Custom optimization algorithms
Scope
Cross-functional, partner integrations
Result
28% lift in conversions

The pattern

When a client splits its spend across several providers, the next dollar goes to whoever proves the best performance. Generalist providers often lose that contest to specialists with purpose-built tools.

Closing the gap takes more than a better pitch. It means building something tailored to the client's goals, and fitting it into the platforms where the client already buys.

My approach

  • Brought every team to one goal. I collaborated with business leads, product, engineering, data science, and our partnerships team around a single objective: earning a greater share of the client's spend.
  • Built custom, not off-the-shelf. We developed optimization algorithms tailored to the client's performance goals, rather than relying on generic models.
  • Integrated where the spend lives. Working with our partnerships team, we built the algorithms to integrate with in-house technology from selected demand-side platforms (DSPs), so the gains landed directly in the client's buying workflow.

Impact

  • A 28% lift in conversions.
  • Performance that outpaced a more specialized competitor.
