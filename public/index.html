01 / About

For 13 years, I've led product across technology, analytics, and enterprise operations for research companies, media organizations, retailers, and consumer brands. In every one, the work comes back to the same question: where is the value we aren't capturing yet?

I'm drawn to the problems that hold back growth: fragmented systems, competing priorities, and good products that never found their audience. I measure success the way the business does, in growth, efficiency, and real outcomes that people can see.

I bring business leaders, engineers, data scientists, and subject-matter experts together behind one plan and see it through. I pair product strategy with hands-on AI development, so the vision and the build stay connected from the first conversation to the final evaluation.
